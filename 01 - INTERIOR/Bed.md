# Bed

## Overview

The main bed is mounted on rails so it can move longitudinally within the ambulance.

## Requirements

- Support two people.
- Comfortable for sleeping.
- Move reliably.
- Not obstruct access to other areas.
- Slim enough to walk under.
- Hand operated.

## Current design

1. Two linear bearing modules attached to each stainless steel pole; there are four poles.
2. Rail mounting and bed-frame details are still being developed.

## Ideas

- Linear rails.
- Telescopic drawer-style slides.
- Custom roller system.

## Open questions

- What load rating is required?
- How much movement is needed?
- How will the rails be mounted?
- How will the bed be secured while travelling?
