# Wardrobe

## Overview

The wardrobe provides hanging and folded-clothes storage for full-time living.

## Requirements

- Enough hanging space for two people.
- Shelves or drawers for folded clothes.
- Secure doors or latches for travel.
- Ventilation to prevent damp and mould.
- Accessible without blocking the bed or walkway.

## Open questions

- Location and dimensions.
- Hanging vs shelf ratio.
- Door type: hinged, sliding or curtain.
- Ventilation path.
- Whether the wardrobe shares space with electrical equipment or tanks.
