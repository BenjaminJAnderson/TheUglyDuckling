# Toilet

## Overview

The toilet will be a **composting / separating toilet**. It should be portable so it can be moved out of the shower compartment when the shower is in use. The model is still to be chosen.

A fixed toilet vent is planned at the **lower section of the back wall** of the shower compartment. There is no black-water tank.

## Requirements

- Separate urine and solids.
- No black-water tank.
- Portable enough to move out of the shower compartment.
- Ventilation to control odour and moisture.
- Easy emptying of the urine container and solids container.
- Stable mounting or docking position when in use.
- Easy cleaning.
- Power for a small extract fan, if required.

## Current design

### Toilet type

A composting / separating toilet separates urine from solids. The solids are covered with a dry material such as sawdust or coco coir and break down over time. The urine is collected separately and emptied.

### Portability

- The toilet is not fixed permanently in the shower compartment.
- It can be moved out when the shower is in use.
- A stable use position and a storage position are needed.
- If the toilet has an integrated fan, it needs a 12 V supply at the use position.
- If the toilet needs a fixed vent, a flexible hose connection or a docking hood may be needed at the use position.

### Ventilation

A fixed vent is planned at the **lower section of the back wall** of the shower compartment.

Options to investigate:

- Integrated fan and carbon filter inside the toilet.
- Flexible hose from the toilet to the fixed wall vent.
- Fixed vent hood at the use position, with the toilet docking underneath.
- Passive vent plus a room extractor.

The final choice depends on the toilet model. The vent should be easy to connect and disconnect when the toilet is moved for showering.

### Urine and solids

- Urine container: portable, with cap and handle, sized for easy emptying.
- Solids container: removable bucket or tray.
- Cover material: sawdust, coco coir or similar, stored next to the toilet.
- Spare container or liner.
- Emptying access: the urine container can be carried out; the solids container needs a route to an external bin or compost point.

### Cleaning and hygiene

- Single-ply toilet paper.
- Small bin for wipes and non-compostables.
- Gloves, sanitiser and cleaning materials.
- Odour absorber or carbon filter if required.
