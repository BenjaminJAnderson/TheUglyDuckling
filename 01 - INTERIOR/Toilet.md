# Toilet

## Overview

The toilet is a **BOXIO TOILET XL MAX+ complete set**, a portable urine-diverting / separating dry toilet. It moves out of the shower compartment when the shower is in use. There is no black-water tank and no dedicated toilet vent.

## Requirements

- Separate urine and solids.
- No black-water tank.
- Portable enough to move out of the shower compartment.
- Compartment ventilation to control odour and moisture; no dedicated toilet vent.
- Easy emptying of the urine canister and solids container.
- Stable use position and retention for travel.
- Easy cleaning.
- No 12 V supply required for the toilet itself.

### How it works

- Urine is diverted forward into the 7 L canister. Solids fall into the 8 L container, usually with a Bio-Bag.
- Hemp litter is sprinkled over the solids to absorb moisture and reduce odour. The toilet does **not** compost the solids in situ.
- Urine can be emptied into a normal toilet. Solids go into general waste in the Bio-Bag; confirm local disposal rules.
- No water or chemicals are used, and there is no black-water tank.
- Odour control relies on the separator, the plug/odour trap, the litter and regular emptying. Keep the lid closed when the toilet is not in use.

### Portability and use position

- The complete set is 40 × 30 cm and 45 cm high, and weighs 5.8 kg. It can be lifted out of the shower compartment.
- The use position and storage position are still to be defined in [[T02 Design toilet use, storage and retention]].
- Plan for the 45 cm height with the Solo Up base. The base can be removed if a lower height is needed, but that changes the storage and seating arrangement.
- Stand the toilet on a level, stable floor. Use a retention strap or non-slip base so it cannot move in transit.
- The 150 kg load is supported by the divider insert, not the lid alone.
- Check the door, threshold and turning space with the toilet in both the use and storage positions.

### Ventilation

- There is **no dedicated toilet vent**. The Boxio has no integrated fan or direct vent port listed.
- The shower compartment extractor and general room ventilation control moisture and odour. Run the extractor after toilet use.
- Do not add a lower-back-wall toilet vent, flexible hose, docking hood, carbon filter or 12 V toilet fan unless the design changes.
- Keep the lid closed, use the litter and empty the containers regularly.

### Urine and solids

- **Urine canister:** 7 L, portable, with cap and handle. Empty into a normal toilet and rinse.
- **Solids container:** 8 L, removable, with a Bio-Bag. Cover the solids with hemp litter and dispose of the bag in general waste.
- A spare 7 L canister allows a swap while one is being emptied.
- Emptying access: the toilet can be carried out of the compartment; the solids bag needs a route to an external bin.
- Consumables: hemp litter, Bio-Bags and Shake. The complete set includes a starter quantity.

### Cleaning and hygiene

- Clean with a low-concentration bicarbonate of soda and/or vinegar solution.
- Check any surface disinfectant for material compatibility before use.
- Use single-ply toilet paper.
- Keep a small bin for wipes and non-compostables.
- Store gloves, sanitiser and cleaning materials near the toilet.
- Avoid harsh chemicals and do not use a toilet additive that could damage the plastic or the disposal route.
