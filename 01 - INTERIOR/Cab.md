# Cab

## Overview

The cab is the driving area of the ambulance. This page covers cab trim, storage, seating and any changes needed for living use.

## Requirements

- Keep the driving position legal and safe.
- Retain access to controls and visibility.
- Add useful storage where possible.
- Insulate and trim the cab consistently with the living area.
- Avoid obstructing airbags and seatbelts.

## Open questions

- How much cab trim and insulation is needed?
- Is a swivel passenger seat wanted?
- What storage can be added without affecting safety?
- How will the cab be separated from the living area for heating?
