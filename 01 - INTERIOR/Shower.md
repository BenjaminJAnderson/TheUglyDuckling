# Shower

## Overview

The shower uses a **Hamwells Loopz Plus** portable recirculating shower. It is installed semi-permanently: the Loopz base forms the shower-compartment floor, the walls are a battened plastic-panel build, and the base slides into place and is sealed with a waterproof rubber trim.

The shower compartment also acts as the **drying room**, heated by a duct from the already-installed Eberspächer Airtronic D4 diesel heater.

## Requirements

- A stable, level floor with a 70 × 70 cm footprint for the Loopz base.
- A **1° fall towards the Loopz drain**.
- Waterproof, wipe-clean walls.
- The Loopz unit must slide in and out for servicing and storage.
- A 12 V DC supply for the Loopz (60 W maximum), with the socket outside the cubicle.
- A way to fill the 6 L reservoir with fresh water.
- A gravity drain from the Loopz to the 45 L grey tank.
- A new roof extractor for steam and moisture.
- A ducted heat supply from the diesel heater, with a changeover switch between the living space and the shower.
- No hot-water plumbing.

## Current design

### Wall build — Option B plastic panels

- **3×2 CLS timber** battens at **300 mm centres**. Nominal 3×2 CLS is roughly 38 × 63 mm finished; check the supplier.
- Battens fixed to the van's structural ribs with rivnuts/plus nuts or structural rivets. The user has confirmed the ribs are suitable.
- Noggins at panel edges, joints, curtain-rail fixings and all fixture points.
- **6 mm solid PVC or compact laminate panels** — mechanically fixed, not adhesive-only.
- Stainless screws with sealing washers; pre-drill and avoid over-tightening.
- Neutral-cure silicone sealant at joints and penetrations.
- H-trims or edge trims at panel joints.
- Allow a few millimetres for thermal expansion at panel edges.
- Insulation behind the panels to reduce condensation.
- Heat shield or non-combustible protection near the diesel-heater outlet.
- Check the panel's fire rating before buying; avoid ordinary thin PVC near the heater.

### Floor and Loopz base

- Level, load-bearing floor support under the full 70 × 70 cm tray.
- **1° fall towards the drain**, built into the base rather than wedging the tray.
- The tray is 10 cm high; allow for a threshold, ramp or recessed floor.
- The Loopz base slides into place.
- A waterproof rubber trim seals the base to the walls and floor.
- Use a removable bead or clamp detail so the unit can still be slid out; do not rely on permanent silicone alone.
- Secure the Loopz with a strap or latch for travel. The safety instructions require horizontal transport and tying down the parts.
- Keep the heater outlet away from the Loopz plastic and electronics.

### Water

- Fill the Loopz reservoir with **6 L** of fresh water before each shower.
- Fill from the kitchen tap using a jug, funnel or short food-grade hose.
- Drain the reservoir after every shower through the Loopz drain plug.
- Drain to the **45 L grey tank** through a floor penetration.
- The underslung tank should give a gravity drain. Confirm a continuous fall from the Loopz outlet to the tank when the tank is sourced.
- Fit a **HepvO or P-trap** to stop grey-tank smells.
- Fit a **grey-tank vent**.
- Use a removable hose coupling and cap so the drain can be cleaned.
- If a continuous fall cannot be achieved, add a small sump/drain pump.

### Hot water

There is no hot-water system. The Loopz does not heat water.

- Shower at ambient water temperature, or
- Preheat water with the kettle and mix it down to **45 °C or below** before filling the Loopz.

### Power

- Dedicated **12 V socket outside the cubicle**, in a dry location.
- Fuse at **7.5–10 A**.
- The Loopz draws about **5 A at 12 V** (60 W maximum).
- Use the supplied 12 V adapter, or provide a hardwired 12 V connection with a suitable plug.
- The manual requires the power adapter to be in a dry environment.
- Isolate the circuit when the unit is removed.

### Ventilation — new roof extractor

- New 12 V extractor fan; do not reuse the existing black roof extractor.
- Humidity sensor or timer, plus a manual boost switch.
- Duct to a new roof vent with a cowl, insect mesh and backdraught shutter.
- Support the duct and insulate it to reduce condensation.
- Provide a passive air inlet so the extractor can move air.
- Dry the compartment after use to prevent mould.

### Drying room — diesel heater

The Eberspächer Airtronic D4 is already mounted. See [[Diesel Heater]].

- **89 mm hot-air duct** into the shower.
- A **changeover damper/vent switch** so the heated air can be directed to either the living space or the shower.
- Keep the main heater outlet open; do not dead-end the heater when the shower branch is closed.
- **76 mm return air** drawn from the dry living space, not from the shower.
- **24 mm combustion air and exhaust** terminate outside.
- Eberspächer 802 digital controller.
- Closable, high-temperature diffuser in the shower.
- High-temperature ducting, clamps and a heat shield.
- CO alarm.
- Suggested drying sequence: squeegee walls → extract 10–15 minutes → heat/circulate with the vent open → short extract to remove moist air.

### Travel and vibration

- Use mechanical fixings into battens and structural ribs; adhesive is secondary.
- Use sealing washers and stainless fixings.
- Allow expansion gaps at panel edges and cover with trims.
- Use flexible sealant, not rigid grout.
- Secure the Loopz, shower head, hose, control panel and deck.
- Support the extractor duct, heater duct and drain hose with clamps.
- Avoid sharp edges, kinks and rubbing points.
- Check fixings after the first few trips.

## Open questions

- Exact 6 mm PVC or compact laminate panel product and fire rating.
- H-trim and edge-trim profiles.
- Extractor fan model and roof-vent location.
- Heater changeover damper/vent type and duct route.
- Confirm Loopz maximum water temperature and weight with Hamwells.
- Confirm the Loopz deck is suitable for the intended users (100 kg limit).
- Confirm the grey-tank fall when the tank is sourced.
- Integrate the toilet's fixed lower-back-wall vent with the shower wall build.
- Final threshold/ramp detail for the 10 cm Loopz base.
