# Shower

## Overview

The shower uses a **Hamwells Loopz Plus** portable recirculating shower. It is installed semi-permanently: the Loopz base forms the shower-compartment floor, the walls are a battened, plywood-backed black PVC cladding build, and the base slides into place and is sealed with a **JA Seals Multi Seal** compression trim fixed to the walls.

The shower compartment also acts as the **drying room**, heated by a duct from the already-installed Eberspächer Airtronic D4 diesel heater.

## Requirements

- A stable, level floor with a 70 × 70 cm footprint for the Loopz base.
- A **1° fall towards the Loopz drain**.
- Waterproof, wipe-clean walls.
- **2 mm black PVC hygienic cladding** on a plywood backing for stiffness and impact resistance.
- The Loopz unit must slide in and out for servicing and storage.
- A removable compression seal fixed to the walls, not the Loopz base.
- A 12 V DC supply for the Loopz (60 W maximum), with the socket outside the cubicle.
- A way to fill the 6 L reservoir with fresh water.
- A gravity drain from the Loopz to the 60 L grey tank.
- A new roof extractor for steam and moisture.
- A ducted heat supply from the diesel heater, with a changeover switch between the living space and the shower.
- No hot-water plumbing.

## Current design

### Wall build — black PVC on plywood backing

- **3×2 CLS timber** battens at **300 mm centres**. Nominal 3×2 CLS is roughly 38 × 63 mm finished; check the supplier.
- Battens fixed to the van's structural ribs with rivnuts/plus nuts or structural rivets. The user has confirmed the ribs are suitable.
- Noggins at panel edges, joints, curtain-rail fixings and all fixture points.
- **Plywood backing panels** fixed to the battens. Thickness and grade are TBC; use a moisture-resistant or marine-grade plywood with sealed edges. The backing provides stiffness and impact resistance for the 2 mm cladding.
- **2 mm black PVC hygienic cladding**, 3050 × 1220 mm sheets, Hygienic Plastic Supplies SKU `B2104`, **£49.99 + VAT per sheet**.
- The supplier states the cladding is **Class 0 fire rated to EN 13501-1 B-s3,d0**, solid PVC (not foam-filled) with a satin finish. Verify the certificate before ordering.
- **Three walls, approximately 1970 × 700 mm.** One 3050 × 1220 mm sheet yields one wall panel, so **three sheets minimum**; expect substantial offcut. Confirm final dimensions and consider the supplier's cut-to-size service.
- Bond the PVC to the plywood with a suitable adhesive and/or fix at the edges with stainless screws and sealing washers. Confirm the fixing detail and avoid unnecessary penetrations in the wet zone.
- H-trims or edge trims at panel joints; neutral-cure silicone at joints and penetrations.
- Allow a few millimetres for thermal expansion at panel edges.
- Insulation behind the plywood to reduce condensation.
- Heat shield or non-combustible protection near the diesel-heater outlet; keep the cladding clear of the outlet.
- Unwrap the PVC on delivery and store it flat; the supplier ships some sheets rolled for transit.

### Floor and Loopz base

- Level, load-bearing floor support under the full 70 × 70 cm tray.
- **1° fall towards the drain**, built into the base rather than wedging the tray.
- The tray is 10 cm high; allow for a threshold, ramp or recessed floor.
- The Loopz base slides into place.
- A **JA Seals Multi Seal 2.5 m** compression trim seals the base to the three walls. It is fixed to the walls only; the base stays free so it can slide out.
- Use the trim as the primary mechanical seal. Seal screw heads and corner joints with neutral-cure silicone; do not bond the trim to the base.
- Secure the Loopz with a strap or latch for travel. The safety instructions require horizontal transport and tying down the parts.
- Keep the heater outlet away from the Loopz plastic and electronics.

#### Base perimeter seal — JA Seals Multi Seal

- **Product:** [JA Seals Multi Seal 2.5 m](https://www.jaseals.co.uk/multi-seal-25m_165), **£24 inc VAT**. It is a garage-door compression seal for gaps up to 25 mm, supplied with double-sided tape and self-tapping screws.
- **Role:** primary mechanical compression seal between the Loopz base and the three walls.
- **Fixing:** fix to the walls only, through the 2 mm PVC cladding into the plywood backing, engaging battens where possible. Pre-drill the cladding, use the supplied screws or stainless equivalents, and do not overtighten. Use the tape for location only.
- **Base removal:** do not screw or bond the trim to the Loopz base. The base must slide out without dismantling the trim.
- **Compression:** position the trim so its flexible lip bears lightly on the base perimeter. Measure the actual gap first; the product covers gaps up to 25 mm, but it must not be compressed so hard that the base cannot be removed.
- **Corners:** mitre or overlap the trim at the two rear corners and seal the joints with neutral-cure silicone.
- **Screw sealing:** seal every screw head with neutral-cure silicone.
- **Material check:** the product page does not state the rubber type, mould resistance or wet-area suitability. Confirm these with JA Seals before ordering, along with the profile drawing and compression range.
- **Length:** three 700 mm walls use 2.1 m, leaving about 0.4 m for corners and offcuts. Measure before cutting; the 3.7 m pack is the fallback if the corner allowance exceeds 2.5 m.
- **Validation:** dry-fit the base and trim, remove and refit the base at least twice, then prove the joint in [[S09 Shower watertightness test]].
- **Fallback:** if the trim does not seal reliably, use a neutral-cure silicone or MS-polymer primary seal with a removable cover trim, or a shower-specific product such as Sealux Aquastrap.

### Water

- Fill the Loopz reservoir with **6 L** of fresh water before each shower.
- Fill from the kitchen tap using a jug, funnel or short food-grade hose.
- Drain the reservoir after every shower through the Loopz drain plug.
- Drain to the **60 L grey tank** through a floor penetration.
- The 60 L Autocraft tank is supplied without fittings and will be drilled for a 3/4" Loopz inlet and a 40 mm sink inlet. Confirm the drill positions, service-hatch orientation and Loopz connection before ordering. See [[Water]].
- The underslung tank should give a gravity drain. Confirm a continuous fall from the Loopz outlet to the tank when the tank is sourced.
- Fit a **HepvO or P-trap** to stop grey-tank smells.
- Fit a **grey-tank vent**.
- Use a removable hose coupling and cap so the drain can be cleaned.
- If a continuous fall cannot be achieved, add a small sump/drain pump.

### Hot water

There is no hot-water system. The Loopz does not heat water.

- Shower at ambient water temperature, or
- Preheat water with the kettle and mix it down to **45 °C or below** before filling the Loopz.

### Power

- Dedicated **12 V socket outside the cubicle**, in a dry location.
- Fuse at **7.5–10 A**.
- The Loopz draws about **5 A at 12 V** (60 W maximum).
- Use the supplied 12 V adapter, or provide a hardwired 12 V connection with a suitable plug.
- The manual requires the power adapter to be in a dry environment.
- Isolate the circuit when the unit is removed.

### Ventilation — new roof extractor

- New 12 V extractor fan; do not reuse the existing black roof extractor.
- Humidity sensor or timer, plus a manual boost switch.
- Duct to a new roof vent with a cowl, insect mesh and backdraught shutter.
- Support the duct and insulate it to reduce condensation.
- Provide a passive air inlet so the extractor can move air.
- Dry the compartment after use to prevent mould.

### Drying room — diesel heater

The Eberspächer Airtronic D4 is already mounted. See [[Diesel Heater]].

- **89 mm hot-air duct** into the shower.
- A **changeover damper/vent switch** so the heated air can be directed to either the living space or the shower.
- Keep the main heater outlet open; do not dead-end the heater when the shower branch is closed.
- **76 mm return air** drawn from the dry living space, not from the shower.
- **24 mm combustion air and exhaust** terminate outside.
- Eberspächer 802 digital controller.
- Closable, high-temperature diffuser in the shower.
- High-temperature ducting, clamps and a heat shield.
- CO alarm.
- Suggested drying sequence: squeegee walls → extract 10–15 minutes → heat/circulate with the vent open → short extract to remove moist air.

### Travel and vibration

- Use mechanical fixings into the battens and structural ribs for the plywood backing; bond the PVC to the plywood with a suitable adhesive and fix the edges as confirmed.
- Use sealing washers and stainless fixings.
- Allow expansion gaps at panel edges and cover with trims.
- Use flexible sealant, not rigid grout.
- Secure the Loopz, shower head, hose, control panel and deck.
- Support the extractor duct, heater duct and drain hose with clamps.
- Avoid sharp edges, kinks and rubbing points.
- Check fixings after the first few trips.

## Open questions

- Plywood backing thickness, grade and fixing method.
- Final wall dimensions and sheet cutting plan for the 2 mm PVC cladding.
- Fire certificate and heater clearance for the black PVC cladding.
- H-trim, edge-trim and adhesive specification.
- Confirm the Multi Seal rubber type, mould resistance, profile and wet-area suitability with JA Seals.
- Measure the Loopz base-to-wall gap and confirm the 2.5 m trim covers it with enough corner allowance.
- Extractor fan model and roof-vent location.
- Heater changeover damper/vent type and duct route.
- Confirm Loopz maximum water temperature and weight with Hamwells.
- Confirm the Loopz deck is suitable for the intended users (100 kg limit).
- Confirm the grey-tank fall when the tank is sourced.
- Integrate the toilet's fixed lower-back-wall vent with the shower wall build.
- Final threshold/ramp detail for the 10 cm Loopz base.
