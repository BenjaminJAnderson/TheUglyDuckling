# Washer & Dryer

## Overview

A washing machine and dryer are being considered for full-time living. They are not part of the current kitchen/shower/toilet phase.

## Requirements

- Low water consumption.
- Low power consumption, or a plan for running from shore power only.
- Secure mounting for travel.
- Drain connection to the grey tank or a suitable container.
- Ventilation for a dryer, or a condensing/ventless design.
- Space for detergent and laundry storage.

## Open questions

- Is a washer worth the water, power and space?
- Washer-only, washer-dryer, or no machine?
- 12 V, 230 V or manual/portable machine?
- Where would it drain?
- Can it run from the planned battery bank and inverter?
