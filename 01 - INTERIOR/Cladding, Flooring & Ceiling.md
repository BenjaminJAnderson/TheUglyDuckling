# Cladding, Flooring & Ceiling

## Overview

This page covers the interior lining, insulation, floor and ceiling. The kitchen and shower area need moisture-resistant and waterproof finishes.

## Requirements

- Insulate the walls, floor and ceiling.
- Reduce thermal bridging.
- Provide a vapour-control layer where appropriate.
- Use moisture-resistant materials in the wet area.
- Provide a waterproof, non-slip shower floor.
- Keep fixing points accessible for future services.
- Maintain headroom and vehicle weight limits.

## Current design

- The shower compartment walls are 3×2 CLS battens at 300 mm centres with 6 mm solid PVC or compact laminate waterproof panels.
- The Loopz base forms the shower floor.
- The kitchen needs wipe-clean walls and a heat-resistant splashback.
- The ceiling needs access for wiring and vents.

## Open questions

- Insulation material and thickness.
- Vapour-barrier strategy.
- Floor substrate and finish.
- Wall-panel material for the kitchen and wet area.
- Ceiling panel material and access hatches.
- How to seal edges and penetrations.
