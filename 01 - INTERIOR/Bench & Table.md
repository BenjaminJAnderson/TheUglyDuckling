# Bench & Table

## Overview

The bench and table form the main seating and dining area. The table design has been developed in FreeCAD and cut on the CNC.

## Requirements

- Comfortable seating for at least two people.
- Table usable for eating, working and food preparation.
- Secure while travelling.
- Does not obstruct access to the kitchen or wet area.
- Storage underneath where possible.

## Current design

- FreeCAD table model: `CAD Files/FreeCAD/Table.FCStd`.
- CNC toolpath: `CAD Files/GCODE/Table.nc`.
- Table legs and stiffener have been cut and fitted.

## Open questions

- Final table height and seating height.
- Whether the table folds, slides or stays fixed.
- Bench storage layout.
- Cushion material and fixing.
- How the table locks for travel.
