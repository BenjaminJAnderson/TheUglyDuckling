# Kitchen, Shower & Toilet

## Overview

This is the entry point for the kitchen, shower and toilet build. The three areas share the water, waste, ventilation and electrical systems, but each has its own detailed page.

## Layout concept

- The **Loopz Plus shower base** forms the floor of the shower compartment.
- The shower compartment walls are 3×2 CLS battens at 300 mm centres with 6 mm solid PVC or compact laminate panels, sealed with a rubber trim around the Loopz base.
- A new **roof extractor** vents the shower.
- The **Eberspächer D4 diesel heater** can be switched between the living space and the shower for drying.
- The **composting toilet is portable** and can be moved out of the shower compartment when the shower is in use. It connects to a fixed vent at the lower back wall.
- The **kitchen is cold-water only**. Hot water comes from a kettle.
- The **induction hob and kettle are limited to 1.5 kW each** and run from the inverter.
