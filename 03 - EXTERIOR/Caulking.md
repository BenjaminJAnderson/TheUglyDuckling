# Caulking

## Overview

Sealing and weatherproofing for roof penetrations, solar mounts, vents and exterior fittings. Good sealing is essential to prevent water ingress and rot.

## Requirements

- UV-stable, flexible sealant.
- Compatible with the roof and panel materials.
- Suitable for vibration and thermal movement.
- Mechanical fixing or flashing where possible, not sealant alone.
- Clean, dry surfaces before application.
- Regular inspection and maintenance.

## Current design

- Roof penetrations are recorded in [[Roof]].
- Solar mounting uses black anodised aluminium extrusion.
- Cable entries should use weatherproof glands with drip loops.
- Future vents need rain caps and insect mesh.

## Open questions

- Which sealant is compatible with the roof coating?
- Which penetrations need mechanical flanges or flashings?
- Inspection interval and maintenance access.
- How to seal around the antenna and solar cables.
