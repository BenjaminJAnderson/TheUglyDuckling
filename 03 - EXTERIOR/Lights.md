# Lights

## Overview

Exterior lighting for the ambulance, including work lights, reversing lights, marker lights and security lighting.

## Requirements

- Legal road lighting.
- Reversing and work lighting.
- Low power consumption.
- Weatherproof and vibration-resistant.
- Separate switching from interior lighting.
- Fused circuits close to the source.

## Open questions

- Which existing lights are retained?
- Additional work lights or scene lights?
- Awning or porch light?
- Security lighting and switching.
- Cable routes and sealing.
