# Roof

## Overview

The metal roof decking was **cancelled**. The roof is now used for the installed 800 W solar array and existing service penetrations. There is **no ladder**.

This page records the roof measurements and penetrations so that solar mounting, venting and future work can be planned around them.

## Solar layout

- 4 × 200 W panels, 800 W total.
- Recorded layout: 3 lengthways and 1 horizontal, leaving space on the right.
- Verify the final installed layout against the measurements above before adding vents or mounts.
- See [[Solar]] for the electrical design.

## Decking and access

- Metal roof decking: **cancelled**.
- Walkable metal grate: **cancelled**.
- Ladder: **cancelled**; see [[Ladder]].
- Roof access for maintenance still needs to be considered, but no permanent ladder is planned.

## Penetrations and sealing

Existing and planned penetrations:

- Black extractor (not reused for the shower).
- White dome box.
- Black nipple.
- Antenna.
- Solar cable entry.
- New shower extractor roof vent (position TBC).
- Diesel heater combustion air and exhaust terminations.
- Future kitchen and toilet vents.

All penetrations must be sealed with a suitable UV-stable sealant and, where possible, a mechanical flashing or cable gland. See [[Caulking]].
