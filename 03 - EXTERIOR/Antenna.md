# Antenna

## Overview

Antenna and connectivity options for mobile internet, TV and radio. The roof already has an antenna penetration; see [[Roof]].

## Requirements

- Reliable mobile internet where possible.
- Weatherproof roof penetration.
- Cable route to the interior.
- No interference with solar panels.
- Secure mounting while driving.

## Open questions

- Mobile router and external antenna choice.
- TV or radio antenna needed?
- Roof or wall mounting.
- Cable entry and sealing.
- Power supply and location of the router.
