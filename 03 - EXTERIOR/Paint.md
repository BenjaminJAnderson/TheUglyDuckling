# Paint

## Overview

Exterior paint and finish for the ambulance. The vehicle is currently in service; paint work is planned around the conversion.

## Requirements

- Suitable for the existing body material and finish.
- UV and weather resistant.
- Compatible with decals or graphics.
- Repairable in sections.
- Preparation and primer suitable for the surface.

## Open questions

- Original paint colour and condition.
- Full respray or touch-up?
- Roller/brush or spray?
- Graphics, signwriting or a plain finish?
- How to protect fresh paint during the build.
