# Solar

## Overview

The ambulance has **800 W of solar** installed as **4 × 200 W panels**. The panels are mounted on black anodised extruded aluminium and are wired **2 in series / 2 in parallel** (2S2P). There is no roof decking and no ladder.

## Requirements

- 800 W total solar capacity.
- Charge the 12 V leisure battery bank.
- Secure mounting at motorway speeds.
- Weatherproof cable entry.
- Isolation and fusing between the panels and the MPPT.
- No roof decking.
- No ladder.

## Current design

- 4 × 200 W black monocrystalline panels.
- Black anodised aluminium mounting extrusion.
- 2S2P array.
- 60 A MPPT controller.
- 6 m × 4 mm² solar extension cables with MC4 connectors.
- Roof penetrations sealed and protected; see [[Roof]] and [[Caulking]].

## Open questions

- Final panel layout and walkway clearance.
- Cable entry point and drip loop.
- DC isolator location.
- Array fusing.
- MPPT mounting location and ventilation.
- Shading from the extractor, antenna or roof penetrations.
- Monitoring and performance logging.
- Whether the panels need tilt or can remain flat.
