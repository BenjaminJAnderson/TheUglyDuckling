# Water

## Overview

The water system supplies the kitchen sink and the Loopz Plus shower, and collects grey water from both. There is **no hot-water system** and **no black-water tank**. The composting toilet collects urine separately.

## Requirements

- 75 L fresh-water storage, underslung.
- 45 L grey-water storage, underslung.
- Cold water at the kitchen sink.
- Fresh-water fill for the Loopz Plus reservoir.
- Grey-water collection from the sink and Loopz drain.
- Drinking-water filtration.
- Tank level monitoring.
- Drain and winterising points.
- Insulation and heating to prevent freezing.
- Easy filling, emptying and maintenance.

## System diagram

```text
Fresh tank (75 L, underslung)
        │
        ▼
   Pump + accumulator
        │
        ▼
   Sediment / carbon filter
        │
        ├──► Kitchen cold tap
        │
        └──► Loopz Plus fill (jug / short hose)

Kitchen sink drain ──► Grey tank (45 L, underslung) ◄── Loopz Plus drain
                              │
                              ▼
                        Grey drain valve

Composting toilet ──► Urine container (separate, portable)
```

## Fresh water

- **Tank:** 75 L, underslung.
- **Fill point:** lockable external fill point with cap.
- **Breather/overflow:** required.
- **Level sensor:** gauge inside the van.
- **Drain valve:** for winterising and maintenance.
- **Pump:** 12 V diaphragm pump.
- **Accumulator:** smooths pressure and reduces pump cycling.
- **Filter:** sediment and carbon for drinking water.
- **Pipework:** PEX or John Guest, with isolation valves.
- **Loopz fill:** 6 L jug or short food-grade hose from the kitchen tap.

The fresh tank is not heated by the engine or a calorifier. There is no hot-water plumbing.

## Grey water

- **Tank:** 45 L, underslung.
- **Sources:** kitchen sink and Loopz Plus drain.
- **Vent:** required to prevent smells and allow draining.
- **Level sensor:** gauge inside the van.
- **Drain valve:** for emptying at a suitable disposal point.
- **Trap and air-admittance valve:** on the sink drain.
- **Loopz drain:** gravity feed through a floor penetration to the underslung tank, with a HepvO or P-trap, a removable coupling and a cap.
- The underslung tank should be below the Loopz outlet. Confirm a continuous fall in the hose when the tank is sourced.
- If a continuous fall cannot be achieved, add a small sump/drain pump.
- The Loopz drains after every shower. Plan the drain hose route so it can be connected and disconnected easily.

## Urine

- The composting toilet uses a separate portable urine container.
- Do not connect urine to the grey tank unless the toilet design specifically allows dilution and disposal.
- Provide a safe, easy route for carrying the container out for emptying.

## Winter protection

The tanks and pipework are underslung and exposed to freezing conditions. Options to investigate:

- Insulated tank enclosure or insulated tank jackets.
- 12 V heater pads with thermostat.
- Heated or insulated pipework.
- Drain valves at low points.
- Drain the fresh system when the van is unused in freezing weather.
- Keep the grey tank drain valve clear and accessible.
- Do not add antifreeze to the fresh-water tank.

The final heating method and power budget are still open.
