# Water

## Overview

The water system supplies the kitchen sink and the Loopz Plus shower, and collects grey water from both. There is **no hot-water system** and **no black-water tank**. The Boxio separating toilet collects urine separately.

## Requirements

- 75 L fresh-water storage, underslung.
- 60 L grey-water storage, underslung.
- Cold water at the kitchen sink.
- Fresh-water fill for the Loopz Plus reservoir.
- Grey-water collection from the sink and Loopz drain.
- Internal gravity topfill, filled over the kitchen sink from 10 L bottles.
- Drinking-water filtration.
- Tank level monitoring.
- Drain and winterising points.
- Insulation and heating to prevent freezing.
- Easy filling, emptying and maintenance.

The change from the previous 75 L fresh / 45 L grey set was driven by the measured underside. The 75 L fresh tank fits. The waste-tank space is awkward: an electrical cable can be removed to free space, but a 400 mm-wide tank still fouls brake-cable and other protrusions. The 60 L tank is **350 mm wide**, so it should clear them, at the cost of a longer 800 mm body.

The 60 L tank is listed as a fresh-water kit but Autocraft states the tank is suitable for either fresh or waste water. It is bought with **no fresh kit and no pump**, so the sink inlet, Loopz inlet, breather and drain fittings must be sourced separately. The tank has one lockable service hatch and no factory-drilled pipe connections; holes are drilled where required and threaded inlets/outlets are fitted with nuts on the inside.

The 60 L Sprinter/Crafter brackets are not a direct fit for the 2019 Ducato/WAS chassis and will need adapting or redrilling. Confirm this is structurally acceptable before ordering.

Topfill was chosen over backfill because it can be filled by gravity from a bottle, funnel or jug; backfill requires an external pressure source. The fill point will be **inside**, in the rear upstand directly behind the kitchen sink, so 10 L bottles can rest on the counter and any spill runs into the sink and grey tank.

**Fitment is not fully confirmed.** The vehicle is a 2019 Ducato with a WAS ambulance box body. The fresh-tank space has been measured and is adequate. The 60 L tank envelope, bracket adaptation, service-hatch orientation and waste-pipe drill positions must be checked before ordering.

The 75 L dimensions calculate to **67.5 L** external but the tank is sold as 75 L; the 60 L dimensions calculate to **70 L** external for a nominal 60 L. Confirm the usable capacity of both with Autocraft. Fittings are reported to protrude by around **60 mm**, so allow clearance beyond the tank envelope.

## Fresh water

- **Tank:** Autocraft 75 L, underslung behind the exhaust box.
- **Fill point:** internal topfill with a black 40 mm lockable filler cap mounted in the rear upstand directly behind the kitchen sink. The cap should sit over the bowl so spills drain into the sink, not behind the worktop.
- **Fill hose:** 40 mm food-grade hose from the cap to the tank inlet, with a continuous fall and no low spots. Support the hose and extend the supplied 1 m length if required. Do not seal a hose into the filler or pressurise the tank.
- **Filling from bottles:** rest a 10 L bottle on the counter and pour through a clean funnel or short food-grade hose into the filler. A jerrycan with a tap is an alternative. Fill slowly and watch the level gauge and breather.
- **Breather/overflow:** 10 mm hose to a high loop and external vent with insect mesh. Route any overflow outside the van, never into the interior. Do not seal the filler.
- **Level sensor:** gauge inside the van. Not included in the kit.
- **Drain:** 1/2" drain outlet; add a 1/2" drain tap because the topfill kit does not include one.
- **Pump:** Shurflo Trail King 7 included in the 75 L kit; self-priming, can run dry, built-in check valve. Mount inside, above the tank.
- **Accumulator:** smooths pressure and reduces pump cycling.
- **Filter:** pump strainer plus a separate sediment and carbon filter for drinking water. Confirm whether the kit “filter” is only a pump strainer.
- **Pipework:** PEX or John Guest, with isolation valves.
- **Loopz fill:** 6 L jug or short food-grade hose from the kitchen tap.

The fresh tank is not heated by the engine or a calorifier. There is no hot-water plumbing.

## Grey water

- **Tank:** Autocraft 60 L, underslung one section back from the fresh tank, using the space freed by removing the electrical cable.
- **Brackets:** Sprinter/Crafter set, adapted and redrilled for the Ducato/WAS chassis. Check the strap or bracket load path after modification.
- **Sources:** kitchen sink and Loopz Plus drain.
- **Inlets:** drill a **40 mm** threaded inlet for the kitchen sink and a **3/4"** threaded inlet for the Loopz drain. Do not reduce a 40 mm sink waste to 3/4" unless the supplier confirms the tank will not back up. Keep fittings clear of the service hatch and the thicker tank corners.
- **Vent:** 3/8" breather to a high loop and external vent with insect mesh.
- **Level sensor:** gauge inside the van. Not included.
- **Drain valve:** 3/4" drain tap at the lowest point. An Autocraft Easy Drain kit (from £11.60) can relocate the tap to a more accessible position.
- **Drain hose:** 3/4" hose to the disposal point; not included with the tank.
- **Trap and air-admittance valve:** on the sink drain.
- **Loopz drain:** gravity feed through a floor penetration to the underslung tank, with a HepvO or P-trap, a removable coupling and a cap.
- **Fall:** the grey tank must be below the Loopz outlet. Confirm a continuous fall in the hose when the tank is fitted. If a continuous fall cannot be achieved, add a small sump/drain pump.
- **Service hatch:** orientation is TBC. The Sprinter listing describes the hatch facing down for that fitment; confirm that this suits a waste tank on the Ducato/WAS and still allows cleaning and draining.

## Filling and draining

### Filling the fresh tank

1. Park level and close the fresh drain tap.
2. Rest a 10 L bottle on the counter. Remove the black lockable cap from the sink upstand and insert a clean funnel or short food-grade hose.
3. Pour slowly by gravity. Do not seal the filler or pressurise the tank. Any spill runs into the sink and grey tank; wipe the worktop afterwards.
4. Watch the level gauge and the 10 mm breather. Stop when the gauge reads full or water appears at the breather.
5. Refit the cap firmly and check for leaks at the filler, hose joints, breather and tank connections.

### Draining the fresh system

1. Turn off the pump and open a kitchen tap to release pressure.
2. Connect a hose to the 1/2" fresh drain tap and route it to an approved disposal point.
3. Open the drain tap and the internal filler cap so air can enter.
4. Drain the pump strainer, accumulator and filter housing.
5. Leave the drain tap and filter housing open for winter storage.

### Draining the grey tank

1. Connect the 3/4" hose to the grey drain tap and route it to an approved disposal point.
2. Open the drain tap slowly. The 3/8" breather should let air in and prevent glugging.
3. Rinse the tank with clean water and drain again.
4. Check the sink trap and Loopz drain for standing water.

### Winterising

- Drain both tanks, pipework, pump strainer, accumulator and filter housing.
- Insulate both tanks and pipework; add thermostatically controlled 12 V heating mats or pads.
- Keep the drain taps accessible; the Easy Drain kit helps for the grey tank.
- Do not add antifreeze to the fresh-water tank.

## Urine

- The Boxio toilet uses a separate portable 7 L urine canister.
- Do not connect urine to the grey tank unless the toilet design specifically allows dilution and disposal.
- Provide a safe, easy route for carrying the container out for emptying.

## Winter protection

The tanks and pipework are underslung and exposed to freezing conditions. Options to investigate:

- Insulated tank enclosure or insulated tank jackets.
- 12 V heater pads or thermal heating mats with a thermostat.
- Heated or insulated pipework.
- Drain valves at low points.
- Drain the fresh system when the van is unused in freezing weather.
- Keep the grey tank drain valve clear and accessible.
- Do not add antifreeze to the fresh-water tank.

The final heating method and power budget are still open. The fresh tank sits behind the exhaust box, so check heat gain as well as frost protection. The 60 L grey tank is longer and narrower than the previous 45 L tank; check that insulation and heater pads fit around the brackets and fittings.
