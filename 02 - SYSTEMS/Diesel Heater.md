# Diesel Heater

## Overview

The ambulance has an **Eberspächer Airtronic D4 12 V Mk1** diesel air heater with an **Eberspächer 802 digital controller**. It is already mounted.

The heater provides space heating and is also ducted to the shower compartment so the shower can be used as a **drying room**.

## Requirements

- Heat the living space.
- Heat the shower compartment for drying.
- Switch the heated air between the living space and the shower.
- Draw return air from the dry living space, not the shower.
- Keep combustion air and exhaust outside.
- Safe clearances from combustible materials.
- CO alarm.
- Service access.

## Current design

### Air distribution

- **89 mm hot-air duct** from the heater.
- A **changeover damper or vent switch** selects:
  - living-space heating, or
  - shower drying.
- The main heater outlet must remain open. Do not dead-end the heater when the shower branch is closed.
- A closable, high-temperature diffuser in the shower.
- High-temperature ducting, clamps and a heat shield.
- Insulate the duct where it passes through cold spaces.

### Return air

- **76 mm return-air duct** from the dry living space.
- Do not draw return air from the shower; moist air through the heater is bad for the unit and reduces drying performance.

### Combustion air and exhaust

- **24 mm combustion-air intake** and **24 mm exhaust** terminate outside.
- Keep terminations away from windows, vents and the shower extractor.
- Check for leaks and corrosion.
- Follow the Eberspächer clearances and installation instructions.

### Controls

- Eberspächer 802 digital controller.
- Thermostat control for the living space.
- Manual changeover for the shower drying branch.
- Consider a separate thermostat or timer for the drying cycle if the 802 sensor is in the living space.

### Electrical

- 12 V supply, fused according to the Eberspächer manual.
- Controller wiring and loom as per the installation.
- Check the heater's current draw against the leisure battery and DC distribution.
- See [[Electrics Overview]].

### Fuel

- Existing fuel supply and pump.
- Check the fuel filter, fuel line and pump mounting.
- Follow the Eberspächer service schedule.

## Safety

- CO alarm is essential.
- Combustion exhaust must never enter the living space or shower.
- Keep the hot-air outlet clear of the Loopz plastic, waterproof panels, curtains and stored items.
- Use a heat shield where the duct or outlet is close to combustible material.
- Do not block the return air or hot-air outlets.
- Do not run the heater with the vehicle in an enclosed space.
- Service the heater regularly and check the exhaust for leaks or corrosion.

## Drying-room sequence

1. Squeegee the shower walls and tray.
2. Run the shower extractor for 10–15 minutes.
3. Switch the heater air to the shower.
4. Run the heater with the diffuser open and a small circulation fan if needed.
5. Finish with a short extract to remove moist air.

## Open questions

- Exact changeover damper/vent type and duct route.
- Length and number of bends in the 89 mm and 76 mm ducts; check against the Eberspächer limits.
- Whether the 802 controller sensor is suitable for the drying cycle.
- Electrical fuse rating and wiring route.
- Fuel filter and pump service history.
- CO alarm location.
- Whether a separate duct thermostat is worthwhile.
