# MaxxFan Dome Plus

## Overview

The **MaxxFan Dome Plus** is a compact 12 V exhaust-only roof or sidewall fan with an integrated LED ring, shortlisted for the shower ceiling. The black LED model is part number **00-03810B**. It is a direct roof-mounted vent: the fan and lid form the roof terminal, so no separate duct, cowl or backdraught shutter is required.

The unit is being considered primarily as a combined shower extractor and light. It is single-speed and manually controlled; the previous humidity sensor/timer requirement was relaxed on 2026-11-16. It remains `shortlisted` until the pre-order checks below are complete.

## Ceiling build-up and sleeve length

The shower ceiling is a dropped ceiling under the 50 mm aluminium sandwich roof:

| Layer | Thickness |
| --- | --- |
| 2 mm PVC cladding and plywood | ~5.6 mm |
| CLS battens / service void | 38 mm |
| Aluminium sandwich roof | 50 mm |
| **Total** | **~93.6 mm (3.69")** |

The fan flange seals to the exterior roof. The garnish ring sits against the shower ceiling, so the sleeve spans the full 93.6 mm.

The manual requires the sleeve to be cut longer than the measured thickness to allow for the garnish-ring depth. For the LED version, add **15/16" (23.8 mm)**:

```text
93.6 mm measured + 23.8 mm garnish allowance = ~117 mm sleeve length
```

Measure the actual build-up before cutting. The manual's full-length sleeve is only used at 6" (152 mm); this installation needs a trimmed sleeve. Dry-fit the fan and sleeve first.

## Installation — roof mount through the shower ceiling

Installation and 12 V wiring should be carried out by a competent person in accordance with applicable codes and the manual.

### 1. Position and structure

- Confirm the chosen location is clear of solar panels, roof ribs, wiring, the diesel-heater duct/diffuser and existing penetrations.
- The lid opens about 100 mm above the roof. Check that it cannot foul a solar panel or roof fitting.
- Check headroom below the fan. The handle protrudes about 78 mm below the ceiling when closed.
- Position the 159 mm cutout between ceiling battens where possible. Add noggins or trimmers around the opening to maintain the ceiling frame.
- Add a plywood mounting ring around the opening to receive the four garnish-ring screws; the 5.6 mm ply/PVC alone is not a reliable fixing.
- Keep the fan and wiring clear of the high-temperature heater outlet.

### 2. Cut the opening

- Cut a 159 mm circular hole through the PVC, plywood, batten void and 50 mm sandwich roof.
- Use a hole saw deep enough for the build-up, or cut from both sides; do not damage the aluminium skins or core.
- Seal the exposed plywood/PVC edge and the exposed sandwich core with a suitable neutral-cure sealant.
- Deburr the roof skin and clean the flange area.

### 3. Dry-fit and trim the sleeve

- Insert the fan and sleeve; measure from the shower ceiling to the fan base.
- Cut the sleeve to the measured thickness plus 23.8 mm (LED) — about 117 mm for the nominal build-up.
- Align the sleeve notch with the wiring. Deburr the cut edge.

### 4. Fit the roof flange

- Apply a continuous strip of butyl tape in the flange groove.
- Orient the fan with the button toward the front of the vehicle and the wires hanging inside.
- Centre the fan in the opening.
- Fit the eight screws at the flange dimples. Pierce the flange at the dimples; do not overtighten or crack the plastic.
- Confirm the screws have adequate purchase in the 50 mm sandwich roof. If the aluminium skin is too thin, use rivnuts or a bonded/riveted mounting plate.
- Remove excess butyl.
- Apply Dicor self-levelling lap sealant or equivalent over every screw head and a bead approximately 3/16" (5 mm) around the flange.
- Do not use sealants containing xylene, toluene, MEK, acetate or acetone, or polysulfide sealants. Confirm compatibility with the aluminium roof coating.
- Fit the rain shield/fan guard only as specified in the manual and parts pack. If a rain shield is used on a sloping roof, face the slots downslope.

### 5. Electrical

- Supply: 12 V DC, 2-core cable, 1.0–1.5 mm² for the short ceiling run.
- Protect the circuit with a **5 A fuse** close to the source. The fan's internal 5 A GMA fuse is not a substitute for circuit protection.
- Provide an isolator or switch outside the cubicle if convenient; the fan's own on/off and LED switches are at the ceiling.
- Use the supplied splices/wire nuts. Connect positive and negative to the fan and LED wires as marked.
- Route the wires outside the sleeve; do not pinch them. Excess wire and connectors sit in the garnish ring.
- Fit a drip loop and a sealed cable entry. Isolate the circuit before working on the fan.

### 6. Interior garnish ring

- Insert the sleeve into the garnish ring with the notch aligned to the wiring.
- Raise the garnish ring to the ceiling and centre it over the opening.
- Secure it with the four screws into the plywood mounting ring.
- Seal the garnish ring to the ceiling with neutral-cure silicone to stop steam entering the ceiling void. Do not use acetic-cure silicone.
- Confirm the removable screen is accessible for cleaning.

### 7. Test

- Open and close the lid; check the push-button lock.
- Run the fan and LED independently; check the 5 A circuit fuse.
- Check for vibration, rattle and excessive noise.
- Water-test the roof seal.
- Confirm the passive air inlet is open and adequate; a sealed compartment will starve the fan.

## Operation and maintenance

- Open the manual lid before switching the fan on. The manual states the fan should **not** be operated with the lid closed.
- Close the lid when the fan is not in use to impede air infiltration.
- Clean with mild soap and water. Remove the four screen screws to clean the screen; switch off first and do not run the fan with the screen removed.
- Replace the internal fuse only with a 5 A GMA fast-acting fuse.
- The LED and fan operate independently.
