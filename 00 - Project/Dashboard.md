## Key facts

- 800 W solar installed: 4 × 200 W, wired 2 series / 2 parallel.
- 12 V leisure system with a 2000 W pure-sine inverter.
- Underslung tanks: 75 L fresh, 45 L grey.
- Composting toilet, portable within the wet area.
- Loopz Plus recirculating shower, semi-permanent installation.
- Shower walls: 3×2 CLS at 300 mm centres with 6 mm PVC/compact laminate panels.
- New roof extractor for the shower.
- Eberspächer D4 diesel heater with 802 controller; changeover between living space and shower drying.
- No hot-water system; kettle only.
- No roof decking and no ladder.
- Battery chemistry and final capacity still to be decided.
