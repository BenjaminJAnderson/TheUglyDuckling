# V04 Install shower extractor and roof vent

## Outcome

The MaxxFan Dome Plus is installed through the shower ceiling and sealed weatherproof.

## Done when

- [ ] 159 mm roof penetration cut and sealed
- [ ] Ceiling opening framed with noggins/plywood mounting ring
- [ ] Sleeve trimmed to the measured build-up plus garnish allowance
- [ ] MaxxFan mounted; flange sealed with butyl tape and lap sealant
- [ ] Rain shield/fan guard fitted as specified
- [ ] Garnish ring sealed to the ceiling
- [ ] Passive air inlet planned or fitted
- [ ] 12 V feed, 5 A fuse and isolator connected
- [ ] Fan, LED and lid tested
- [ ] Roof sealed and checked for leaks

## Notes

- The MaxxFan is a direct roof mount; there is no separate duct, cowl or backdraught shutter.
- The nominal build-up is ~93.6 mm; trim the sleeve to about 117 mm (93.6 mm + 23.8 mm LED garnish allowance). Measure before cutting.
- Avoid the solar panels, roof ribs and existing penetrations; check the lid's ~100 mm opening clearance.
- Seal the ply/PVC cut edge and the exposed sandwich core.
- Use a drip loop and sealed cable entry.
- See [[MaxxFan Dome Plus]] for the construction checklist.
