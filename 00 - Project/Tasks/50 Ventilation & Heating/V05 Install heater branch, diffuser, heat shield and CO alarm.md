# V05 Install heater branch, diffuser, heat shield and CO alarm

## Outcome

The diesel heater can supply either the living space or the shower safely.

## Done when

- [ ] 89 mm duct run from the heater
- [ ] Changeover damper fitted and labelled
- [ ] Closable diffuser fitted in the shower
- [ ] Heat shield fitted near the outlet
- [ ] 76 mm return-air route confirmed from the dry living space
- [ ] CO alarm fitted and tested
- [ ] Main heater outlet remains open
- [ ] Duct supported, insulated and clear of combustibles

## Notes

- Check duct length and bends against the Eberspächer limits.
- Keep the heater outlet clear of the Loopz and panels.
