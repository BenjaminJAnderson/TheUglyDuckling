# W01 Select tanks, dimensions and mounting points

## Outcome

The Autocraft 75 L fresh and 60 L waste tanks are confirmed for the 2019 Ducato/WAS chassis, with mounting points, internal fill point and drain routes chosen.

## Done when

- [x] 75 L fresh-tank space measured and confirmed under the vehicle
- [ ] 60 L waste-tank envelope confirmed in the space freed by removing the cable, including brake-cable clearance and the 800 mm length
- [ ] Electrical cable identified, isolated, confirmed no longer required and removed
- [ ] Sprinter/Crafter bracket adaptation for the Ducato/WAS chassis confirmed
- [ ] Internal fill point in the rear upstand behind the kitchen sink and the 40 mm fall route confirmed
- [ ] Waste-tank inlet positions, service-hatch orientation and drain point confirmed with Autocraft
- [ ] Tank protection from road debris and exhaust heat planned
- [ ] [[Water]] BOM and [[Decisions]] updated

## Notes

- Selected for planning on 2026-11-04:
  - Fresh: Autocraft 75 L kit, topfill black cap, universal brackets, pump/filter, SKU `75KT-TFB-PU-UB-WOO`, £252.40.
  - Waste: Autocraft 60 L tank with Sprinter/Crafter brackets, no kit, no pump, SKU `60SPCRKT-00-00-60MB-WOO`, £205.00.
- Combined price is £457.40 before separately sourced waste fittings.
- The 75 L fresh tank is 300 × 1000 × 225 mm and fits behind the exhaust box.
- The 60 L waste tank is 350 × 800 × 250 mm. The previous 400 mm-wide tank fouled brake-cable and other protrusions; the narrower tank should clear them once the cable is removed.
- The 60 L tank is longer than the old 45 L tank; confirm the 800 mm length, service-hatch access and protection from road debris.
- The 60 L Sprinter/Crafter brackets are not a direct Ducato/WAS fit and will need adapting or redrilling. Check the load path and fixing strength after modification.
- The 60 L tank is supplied without a fresh kit. Plan separate 40 mm sink and 3/4" Loopz inlets, a 3/8" breather and a 3/4" drain tap.
- The fresh fill point is internal: black lockable cap in the rear upstand directly behind the kitchen sink, filled from 10 L bottles resting on the counter. Spills run into the sink.
- The 40 mm fill hose must fall continuously from the upstand to the tank. The supplied 1 m length may need extending.
- The 10 mm breather/overflow must terminate outside the van, not inside.
- Confirm actual usable capacity: the 75 L external envelope calculates to 67.5 L and the 60 L envelope to 70 L.
- Fittings protrude by around 60 mm; allow clearance beyond the tank envelope.
- The fresh tank sits behind the exhaust box; check heat gain and fit a heat shield if needed.
