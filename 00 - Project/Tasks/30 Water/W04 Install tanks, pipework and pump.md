# W04 Install tanks, pipework and pump

## Outcome

The tanks, pump, filter and pipework are installed and ready for testing.

## Done when

- [ ] Adapted Autocraft brackets and underslung tanks fitted
- [ ] 60 L waste tank drilled for 40 mm sink inlet, 3/4" Loopz inlet, 3/8" breather and 3/4" drain
- [ ] Internal black lockable cap in the sink upstand and 40 mm fill hose with continuous fall fitted
- [ ] 10 mm breather/overflow with high loop and external vent fitted
- [ ] 1/2" fresh drain tap and 3/4" grey drain tap fitted
- [ ] Pump, accumulator and strainer fitted
- [ ] Sediment and carbon filter fitted
- [ ] PEX or John Guest pipework run with isolation valves
- [ ] Level sensors and gauges fitted
- [ ] Heat shield, tank insulation and thermal heating mats fitted
- [ ] Pipework insulated where required

## Notes

- Mount the fresh tank with the fittings clear of the exhaust; allow for the fittings protruding by around 60 mm.
- Identify, isolate, confirm it is no longer required and remove the electrical cable before fitting the 60 L tank.
- Adapt or redrill the Sprinter/Crafter brackets for the Ducato/WAS chassis, then check the fixing load path and strap tension after the first trip.
- Drill the 60 L tank clear of the corners and service hatch; keep the inlets above the waterline and the drain at the lowest point.
- Fit the internal black filler cap in the rear upstand behind the sink. Run the 40 mm fill hose with a continuous fall, support it and avoid low spots that could trap water.
- Route the 10 mm breather/overflow to a high loop and an external vent with insect mesh. Do not terminate it inside the van.
- Fit the thermal heating mats to the tank surfaces recommended by the manufacturer; protect the wiring, thermostat and connections from water and road debris.
- Keep the fresh and grey drain taps accessible; use the Easy Drain kit for the grey tank if the tap is hard to reach.
- Leave access for tank removal, hatch opening and cleaning.
- Confirm the grey-tank fall before closing the floor.
- Do not pressurise the fresh tank when filling; the internal topfill is gravity-fed.
