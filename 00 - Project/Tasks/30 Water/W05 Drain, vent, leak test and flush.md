# W05 Drain, vent, leak test and flush

## Outcome

The fresh and grey water systems are proven leak-free and drain correctly.

## Done when

- [ ] Fresh tank filled through the internal topfill from 10 L bottles and system flushed
- [ ] Fill-point spill path into the sink and 40 mm hose fall checked
- [ ] Pump pressure and accumulator checked
- [ ] Strainer and filter flow checked
- [ ] All joints leak-tested
- [ ] Fresh tank drained through the 1/2" drain tap
- [ ] Grey tank filled from the kitchen sink and Loopz drain, then drained through the 3/4" tap
- [ ] Breathers checked for flow, high loop and insect mesh
- [ ] Vents and traps checked for smell and flow
- [ ] Level sensors checked
- [ ] Winter drain procedure tested and recorded
- [ ] Results recorded in [[M07 Systems commissioned]]

## Notes

- Test the kitchen and shower drains separately and together.
- Check the grey-tank vent with the trap in place.
- Fill the fresh tank by gravity only through the internal sink-upstand filler; do not seal the filler or pressurise the tank.
- Check that spills at the filler run into the sink and that the breather/overflow discharges outside.
- Check the fresh tank, pump strainer, accumulator and filter housing all drain fully.
- Check the grey tank drains without glugging and that the 3/8" breather is clear.
