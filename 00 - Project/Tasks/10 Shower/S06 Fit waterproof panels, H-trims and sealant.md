# S06 Fit plywood backing, PVC cladding, H-trims and sealant

## Outcome

The shower walls are closed with plywood backing and 2 mm black PVC cladding, trimmed and sealed.

## Done when

- [ ] Plywood backing cut, sealed and fixed to the battens
- [ ] PVC cladding cut and dry-fitted
- [ ] PVC bonded and/or fixed using the confirmed detail
- [ ] Expansion gaps allowed at edges
- [ ] Stainless screws and sealing washers fitted where required
- [ ] H-trims and edge trims fitted
- [ ] Neutral-cure silicone applied at joints and penetrations
- [ ] Heat shield fitted near the heater outlet
- [ ] Photos recorded in [[Shower]]

## Notes

- Bond the PVC to the plywood with a suitable adhesive and fix the edges as confirmed; do not rely on silicone alone.
- Unwrap the PVC on delivery and store it flat; the supplier ships some sheets rolled for transit.
- Pre-drill and avoid over-tightening the fixings.
- Do not use acetic-cure silicone.
- Keep the cladding clear of the diesel-heater outlet and heat shield.
