# S07 Fit Loopz base, Multi Seal trim, travel strap and threshold

## Outcome

The Loopz base slides into place, is sealed around its perimeter and can still be removed for servicing.

## Done when

- [ ] Base dry-fitted and clearance checked
- [ ] Base-to-wall gap measured and within the trim's rated gap of up to 25 mm
- [ ] JA Seals Multi Seal fixed to the walls only
- [ ] Screw heads and corner joints sealed with neutral-cure silicone
- [ ] Base removed and refitted at least twice without disturbing the trim
- [ ] Loopz travel strap or latch fitted
- [ ] Threshold or ramp finished
- [ ] Base secured for travel
- [ ] Removal procedure tested and recorded

## Notes

- Use one JA Seals Multi Seal 2.5 m as the primary mechanical compression seal.
- Fix the trim to the walls only, through the PVC cladding into the plywood backing and, where possible, the battens. Do not screw or bond it to the Loopz base.
- Dry-fit the base first. The trim must bear lightly on the base, not clamp it so tightly that it cannot be removed.
- Seal every screw head and the corner joints with neutral-cure silicone.
- Confirm the trim's rubber type, mould resistance and wet-area suitability with JA Seals before ordering.
- Keep the heater outlet clear of the Loopz plastic and electronics.
