# S05 Install wall frame, noggins and insulation

## Outcome

The shower walls are framed at 300 mm centres, fixed to the structural ribs, insulated and ready for first-fix services and plywood-backed PVC cladding.

## Done when

- [ ] Battens fixed to structural ribs with approved fixings
- [ ] Noggins fitted at panel edges, joints and fixture points
- [ ] Insulation fitted behind the plywood line
- [ ] Service routes and penetrations marked
- [ ] Heat-shield backing planned near the heater outlet
- [ ] Frame checked for plumb, level and square

## Notes

- Nominal 3×2 CLS is roughly 38 × 63 mm finished; check the supplier.
- The plywood backing panels will be fixed over the battens before the 2 mm PVC cladding is fitted; confirm the backing thickness and fixing detail in [[S02 Select shower panel product and fire rating]].
- First-fix water, electrical and ventilation routes must pass before the plywood and cladding close the wall.
