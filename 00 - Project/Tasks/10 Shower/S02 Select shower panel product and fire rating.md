# S02 Select shower panel product and fire rating

## Outcome

The 2 mm black PVC cladding, plywood backing and fire rating are confirmed, with fixing requirements and a sheet cutting plan.

## Done when

- [x] Panel product and supplier selected
- [x] Supplier fire rating documented (Class 0, EN 13501-1 B-s3,d0)
- [ ] Fire certificate obtained and verified
- [ ] Plywood backing thickness and grade confirmed
- [ ] Adhesive/fixing detail and trim compatibility confirmed
- [ ] Sheet cutting plan for three walls at approximately 1970 × 700 mm confirmed
- [ ] Weight and expansion behaviour checked
- [ ] [[Shower]] BOM and [[Decisions]] updated

## Notes

- Product: Hygienic Plastic Supplies black PVC, 3050 × 1220 × 2 mm, SKU `B2104`, £49.99 + VAT per sheet.
- Three walls at approximately 1970 × 700 mm; one sheet yields one wall panel, so three sheets minimum with substantial offcut.
- Plywood backing provides stiffness and impact resistance. Thickness and grade are TBC; use moisture-resistant or marine-grade plywood with sealed edges.
- The supplier states Class 0 fire rated to EN 13501-1 B-s3,d0. Verify the certificate and keep the cladding clear of the diesel-heater outlet.
- The product was out of stock at the time of research; confirm lead time before ordering.
- This task gates the shower kit order.
