# S08 Fit shower fittings, drain and HepvO

## Outcome

The shower is usable and drains to the grey tank without smells.

## Done when

- [ ] Shower head, hose and controls fitted
- [ ] IP65 light and switch fitted
- [ ] 12 V socket outside the cubicle fitted and fused
- [ ] Curtain, rail, mirror, hooks and grab handle fitted
- [ ] Drain hose connected through a HepvO or P-trap
- [ ] Removable coupling and cap fitted
- [ ] Grey-tank connection checked

## Notes

- Fuse the Loopz circuit at 7.5–10 A.
- Keep the power adapter in a dry location outside the cubicle.
