# S09 Shower watertightness test

## Outcome

The shower compartment is proven watertight and drains correctly.

## Done when

- [ ] 24-hour water test completed
- [ ] Base trim, panel joints and penetrations checked
- [ ] Multi Seal compression, screw heads and corner seals checked
- [ ] No water behind the trim after the test
- [ ] Base removed and refitted after the test without disturbing the seal
- [ ] Drain flow checked
- [ ] Grey-tank connection checked for leaks
- [ ] Any leaks repaired and retested
- [ ] Results recorded in [[M05 Shower watertight]]

## Notes

- Test before the first shower use.
- Check the Multi Seal compression under load and the Loopz travel strap or latch.
- Remove and refit the base after the test, then recheck the joint.
