# E04 Install battery bank, main fuse, isolation and busbars

## Outcome

The battery bank and high-current distribution are safely installed.

## Done when

- [ ] Battery bank mounted and secured
- [ ] Main fuse fitted close to the battery positive
- [ ] Isolation switch fitted
- [ ] DC busbars fitted
- [ ] Ventilation and restraint checked
- [ ] High-current cables made off and labelled
- [ ] Photos recorded before connecting loads

## Notes

- Keep 230 V AC and water separated.
- Do not energise the system until the charger and protection are installed.
