## Notes

- The **BOXIO TOILET XL MAX+ complete set** is selected at **£249.99** (regular £269.99, VAT included).
- It has **7 L urine / 8 L solids**, a **40 × 30 cm** footprint, **45 cm** height with the Solo Up base, **5.8 kg** weight and a **150 kg** maximum load.
- The Boxio has no integrated fan or direct vent port. The dedicated lower-back-wall vent was removed; the compartment extractor provides ventilation.
- The product page showed both SOLD OUT and in stock with 4–6 working days delivery. Confirm stock and lead time in [[T03 Order toilet, refills and spares]] before ordering.
- The linked manual covers the standard TOILET MAX and lists 5 L / 5.7 L. Confirm the XL's 7 L / 8 L figures with Boxio.
