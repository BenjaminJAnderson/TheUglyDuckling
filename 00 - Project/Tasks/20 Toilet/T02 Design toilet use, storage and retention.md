# T02 Design toilet use, storage and retention

## Outcome

The use position, storage position and retention method are defined for the Boxio toilet.

## Done when

- [ ] 45 cm height, 40 × 30 cm footprint and turning space checked
- [ ] Use and storage positions marked
- [ ] Retention strap or non-slip base method chosen
- [ ] Emptying and cleaning access confirmed
- [ ] Compartment ventilation path confirmed; no dedicated toilet vent
- [ ] [[Toilet]] and [[Ventilation & Moisture]] updated

## Notes

- The Boxio has no direct vent port, so there is no lower-back-wall vent, docking hood, hose, carbon filter or 12 V toilet fan.
- Plan for the 45 cm height with the Solo Up base; the toilet alone is 33 cm high.
- The complete set weighs 5.8 kg and must remain portable enough to leave the shower compartment.
- Keep the lid closed and rely on the plug/odour trap, hemp litter and compartment extractor for odour control.
- Check the toilet cannot move in transit.
